
# India Meteorological Department (IMD) Weather Warning Levels

Source: India Meteorological Department (IMD), Government of India.
Official website: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/

## 1. Colour-coded warning levels

IMD uses four colour-coded warning levels to communicate expected weather hazards and the action people should take.

- Green — No Warning: No action is needed.
- Yellow — Watch: Be aware and keep yourself updated.
- Orange — Alert: Be prepared to take action.
- Red — Warning: Take action.

A red warning means that action should be taken. It should not automatically be described as a "Red Alert".

## 2. Rainfall intensity categories

IMD uses the following rainfall intensity categories:

- Light rainfall: 2.5–15.5 mm
- Moderate rainfall: 15.6–64.4 mm
- Heavy rainfall: 64.5–115.5 mm
- Very heavy rainfall: 115.6–204.4 mm
- Extremely heavy rainfall: 204.5 mm or more

These values are rainfall amounts in millimetres. They should not be confused with hourly rainfall rates.

## 3. Spatial distribution of rainfall

IMD uses these terms to describe how widely rainfall is distributed across reporting stations:

- Isolated: 1–25% of stations.
- Scattered: 26–50% of stations.
- Fairly widespread: 51–75% of stations.
- Widespread: 76–100% of stations.

## 4. Probabilistic forecast terminology

IMD uses the following probability categories:

- Unlikely: Less than 25%.
- Likely: 25–50%.
- Very likely: 50–75%.
- Most likely: More than 75%.

These describe the probability of occurrence, not the intensity of rainfall.

## 5. Warning validity

IMD daily forecasts and warnings generally use a validity period from 08:30 IST on the specified day until 08:30 IST on the following day.

Always check the validity period and date associated with a warning.

## 6. Important guidance for WeatherGPT

- Explain warning colours accurately.
- Do not confuse probability with rainfall intensity.
- Do not treat a general warning explanation as a live warning for a city.
- For current warnings, use the latest official IMD information.
- For current weather and forecasts, use WeatherGPT's live weather database rather than this static document.

# Flood safety guidance for users in India

Source: Directorate General Fire Services, Civil Defence & Home Guards, Ministry of
Home Affairs, Government of India.
Source URL: https://www.dgfscdhg.gov.in/floods
Reference captured: 2 November 2026.

## Before and during possible flooding

- Monitor official weather forecasts and local government instructions.
- Flash flooding can happen quickly. If there is a possibility of flash flooding,
  move to higher ground immediately; do not wait for an instruction if conditions
  are becoming dangerous.
- Be especially cautious near streams, drainage channels, low-lying roads, and areas
  known to flood suddenly.
- If evacuation is instructed, follow local authorities and move to a safer location.
- If instructed and it is safe to do so, switch off utilities at the main switches.
  Never touch electrical equipment while wet or standing in water.

## Avoid these actions

- Do not walk through moving floodwater.
- Do not drive into flooded roads or underpasses.
- Do not touch electrical equipment or fallen power lines in or near water.
- Do not assume a road is safe because the water looks shallow.
- Do not return to a flooded area until authorities say it is safe.
